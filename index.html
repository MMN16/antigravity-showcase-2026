The Psychology of the Singapore Wallet

How do humans behave when the world shakes? Across 40 years of Singapore government data, consumer spending decouples into three primal instincts: Fear, Flexing, and the Lipstick Paradox.

🧻
Mode 01 • Fear Instinct
The Panic Hoarder
+53.9%

When existential dread hits, social status dies instantly. In April 2020 (Circuit Breaker), supermarket sales soared to all-time highs while luxury dropped off a cliff.

Observed in: FairPrice, Sheng Siong (Rice, Canned Food, Dettol)
💎
Mode 02 • Ego Recovery
The Revenge Flexer
+615.5%

Two years of sensory deprivation created explosive pent-up demand. The moment lockdowns lifted, luxury watches and jewellery rebounded by over 600%!

Observed in: Rolex, The Hour Glass, MBS Boutiques
💄
Mode 03 • Coping Mechanism
The Lipstick Pragmatist
+6.1% in GFC

Can't afford a condo downpayment or a COE car? Humans don't stop spending—we trade down to affordable micro-dopamine splurges like cosmetics and specialty coffee.

Observed in: Sephora, Watsons, Premium Skincare
